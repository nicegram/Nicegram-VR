Privacy Policy

Nicegram VR for Meta Quest · effective 29 September 2026

Nicegram VR is an unofficial, open-source Telegram client for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S, published by Appvillis. This policy covers the application. It does not cover Telegram: your account, your messages and your contacts live on Telegram’s servers under Telegram’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which we neither set nor change.

The short version. Telegram carries your messages and calls. If you use experimental group rooms, our room service verifies your Nicegram account and manages room membership. Optional dictation sends its recording to the speech-recognition service you configure. We add no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting of our own. Public Telegram channels show Telegram’s own sponsored messages, which Telegram selects and serves under its policy and which Telegram requires every client to display.

Group rooms and Nicegram verification

Rooms require an existing Nicegram account. When you request verification, the room service receives your Telegram user ID and checks registration with the Nicegram API. You confirm a one-time session in the Nicegram authentication bot. The service compares the confirmed account with your Telegram account before admitting you.

The room service runs on DigitalOcean in Frankfurt. It processes your Telegram user ID, verified display name, Telegram group identifier, room and participant identifiers, temporary access tokens and presence timestamps. Other room participants can see your display name and presence. Protect invitation links: they provide room access together with Nicegram verification.

The existing account API returns a broader profile response. The room service uses the registration result and verified identity; it does not retain balances, unrelated profile fields or the general Nicegram login token, or send them to the headset. It does not receive Telegram login codes, Telegram session files, chat history, messages or call audio.

Room and verification state is held in process memory, not a room database. Verification challenges expire after five minutes; room identities and rooms expire after two hours, and a room nobody is in is removed after five minutes. Participant presence expires after thirty seconds without a heartbeat and is removed on the next cleanup, normally within ten more seconds. Leaving removes your active room membership; it does not delete your Nicegram account or immediately erase the separate verification identity. Restarting the service clears its in-memory state.

DigitalOcean, our hosting provider, processes connection metadata such as IP addresses under its privacy policy. The room service keeps your IP address in memory for about a minute (never longer than seventy seconds), only to limit the rate of requests. Its own log records the kind of request, the result code and the time taken; it does not record IP addresses, Telegram or Nicegram identifiers, room or participant identifiers, names or tokens. Account records held by Nicegram are governed by Nicegram’s own privacy policy.

An invitation you choose to send to a group is an ordinary Telegram message: it is stored by Telegram and visible to the members of that group.

Where your data actually goes

Data destinations depend on the feature you use.

  • Telegram. Signing in, reading and sending happen against Telegram’s API exactly as they do in the official client. Telegram’s policy governs everything that arrives there.
  • Nicegram and DigitalOcean. Account confirmation and room presence, only if you use rooms, as described above. Room chat and call audio stay in Telegram.
  • Your device. Settings this app adds — the silence profile, the interface size, the layout, the folder it opens in — are stored in the app’s private storage on the headset. They are never written to your Telegram account, which is deliberate: an account-level mute would also silence the phone in your pocket.
  • A speech-recognition service you name. Only if you enter one. See below.

Microphone

The app requests microphone access for voice messages, dictation and Telegram calls you choose to join. Room calls start muted; turning the microphone on is a separate action.

  • No wake word. Recording and speaking into a call require your action. Hiding or closing the room panel turns the microphone off, and it stays off until you turn it on again. While a room call is live, voice and video messages cannot be recorded.
  • Dictation is off until you configure it. No recognition service is built in. You supply the address and, if it needs one, a token; both are stored on the device only.
  • A dictation recording goes to your configured recognition address, over HTTPS. The app refuses to send it over plain http to anywhere but this device, because a recording of your voice and your access token would otherwise cross the network in the clear.
  • Recognised text is never sent on its own. It appears for you to read, and you decide whether it is sent.
  • What that service does with the audio is governed by its policy, not this one. Choose it accordingly.

Notifications

Horizon OS has no Google Play Services, so this app has no push notifications. Messages and calls reach you only while the app is running. Nothing arrives while it is closed; what accumulated is shown the next time you open it. This is a property of the platform, stated on the app’s first screen.

Third-party code

The app is a fork of Telegram for Android and inherits dependencies. It includes no Meta SDK and no advertising or analytics SDK:

  • Firebase is removed from this build. Upstream ships Firebase Cloud Messaging, and its initialiser is a content provider that would start on every launch. The Quest build removes the initialiser, the messaging service and the registration receiver from its manifest, so no Firebase component runs and no installation is registered with Google.
  • Google Play Services libraries are still compiled in, inherited from upstream — sign-in, Cast and vision classes among them. Horizon OS has no Play Services, so none of them can execute; the keys that would activate Google sign-in are cleared in this build. They are inert code, not a data path.

Children

This app is not directed at children. Telegram requires its users to meet its own minimum age; group rooms additionally require an existing Nicegram account. This client has no separate age-verification flow.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the headset, including the silence profile and any dictation address or token. Data held by Telegram is deleted through Telegram — see Telegram’s account deletion. For Nicegram account or room-data questions, contact support@appvillis.com. The room-service memory limits above do not delete your Nicegram account or Telegram data.
