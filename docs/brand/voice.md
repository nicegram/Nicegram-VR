# Voice

Preserve the tone selected in store/listing-en.md before this release review:
calm, concise, concrete, with no superlatives or claims of Store approval.
The existing WORK-018 scenarios describe headset users who want to read and answer
without removing the headset. Losing a draft or exposing another account's content
matters more than an entertaining sentence.
