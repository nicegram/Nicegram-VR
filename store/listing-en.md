## Long description

```
Nicegram VR is an unofficial, open-source Telegram client built for Meta Quest.

It is not made or endorsed by Telegram. You sign in to your own Telegram account,
and your messages, contacts and chats stay on Telegram's servers under Telegram's
terms.

WHAT IT IS FOR

A headset is somewhere you went to concentrate. Answering one message should not
mean taking it off — and it should not mean being interrupted by everything else
either.

QUIET BY DEFAULT

Notifications are off when you start. Messages still sync while the app runs. Notifications appear only when you allow them. Turn them on from the chat list header, and your own exceptions decide
what may interrupt: a named person, a named chat, a named word. Everything else
waits in a digest you read when you choose to.

Nothing this app does changes your Telegram account's own notification settings.
Silencing the headset does not silence the phone in your pocket.

ANSWER BY VOICE

Dictation turns speech into text you can edit. Review the text and press Send
to post it to the chat. The recognition service is yours to configure —
no recognition provider is built in. Start dictation to record, then stop to send
the audio to that service. The recording also stops at the time limit.

BUILT FOR A PANEL

The interface is sized for reading at arm's length and for pointing with a ray
rather than a fingertip. One thing at a time: the chat list, or the chat you
opened.

OPEN SOURCE

Every line is published at github.com/nicegram/Nicegram-VR under GPL-2.0. You can
read what runs on your device, build it yourself, and check that the two match.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

This build has no push delivery. Messages and calls can reach it while it is
running; it cannot wake for new messages while closed.
```

Controllers and hand tracking (ray pointing). No gamepad requirement.
