## Long description

```
Nicegram VR is an unofficial Telegram client for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. Sign in with your Telegram account to read and answer chats from a headset panel. It is not made or endorsed by Telegram.

QUIET BY DEFAULT
Notifications start off. Choose which people, chats or words may interrupt you; other messages wait in a local digest. These settings do not change notifications on your phone.

DICTATION
Use a speech-recognition service you configure to turn recorded speech into editable text. Review it and press Send yourself. No recognition provider is included.

EXPERIMENTAL GROUP ROOMS
Create an invitation room for a Telegram group or join one through an invitation. Rooms require an existing Nicegram account and confirmation through the Nicegram authentication bot. Start or join the group's Telegram call from the room, subject to Telegram permissions. Call audio uses Telegram. Rooms are temporary; persistent worlds, avatars and room editing are not included.

LIMITS
This build has no push delivery while closed. Messages and calls reach it while it runs. Internet access is required for messaging and rooms.

SOURCE
Client source is available at github.com/nicegram/Nicegram-VR under GPL-2.0. Third-party SDKs have their own licenses.
```
